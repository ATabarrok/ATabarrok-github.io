About

Alexander Taghi Tabarrok is a Canadian-American economist whose work spans healthcare policy, innovation economics, and institutional design.

As the Bartley J. Madden Chair in Economics at the Mercatus Center and Professor of Economics at George Mason University, Dr. Tabarrok has established himself as a leading voice in policy-relevant economic research.

His research has appeared in prestigious journals including Science, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, the Journal of Human Resources, and the Journal of Law and Economics. His work has been featured in the New York Times, Washington Post, and Wall Street Journal.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Dr. Tabarrok served as an advisor to the U.S. government, helping develop strategies to accelerate vaccine development and production through innovative incentive mechanisms.

Research Areas

Healthcare Policy

Analyzing the economics of healthcare systems, FDA pharmaceutical regulation, and innovative approaches to increasing organ transplant supply.

Innovation & Patents

Examining patent system reform, the economics of innovation, and how intellectual property policies affect technological progress.

Law & Economics

Investigating how judicial elections influence judge behavior, the effectiveness of bounty hunters, and how local poverty affects jury decisions.

Pandemic Response

Developing economic models for vaccine portfolio allocation and advising on incentive mechanisms to accelerate vaccine development.
